Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Vicinic ("we", "us") operates this website. This page explains what information we collect when you use it, and how we use it. We keep this simple: we don't sell your data, and we don't collect more than we need.

Information we collect

The only personal information we collect directly is what you choose to give us through the contact form on this site — your name, email address, and message. We use a third-party form service (Web3Forms) to deliver that message to us by email; we don't otherwise store form submissions on our own servers.

How we use it

We use the information you submit only to respond to your enquiry — for example, to give you a quote or answer a question about our services. We don't add you to a mailing list or share your details with anyone else without your permission.

Cookies & analytics

This site does not currently use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. If that changes, we'll update this page.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it. Get in touch using the contact form on the Contact section.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us via the contact form.
