There are thousands of beautiful HTML templates available, but they're just static files. To use one on a real platform you have to split out the shared header and footer, fix every asset link and rewire every page link by hand. Templify does that automatically.

## What it does

Templify takes a ready-made website design and turns it into a working, editable site on our platform: shared headers and footers, images, page links and contact forms all come across automatically, for almost any multi-page design.

## Where it's used

Templify is how new designs reach the Vicinic template library quickly, so customers get more choice without waiting on hand-built conversions.
