Summary: Social media pages are rented space. A website on your own domain is the one place online you fully own — here's why it matters and how to get one without breaking the bank.

Most small businesses start online with a Facebook page or an Instagram profile. That's a great first step — but it's rented space. The platform decides who sees your posts, can change the rules overnight, and makes it hard for customers who aren't on that app to find you.

## A website works for you around the clock

A simple website answers the questions customers ask before they buy:

- **What do you do?** A clear headline and a few words about your services.
- **Can I trust you?** Photos of your work, reviews and your contact details.
- **How do I reach you?** A contact form, your phone number and a WhatsApp button.

When someone Googles your business name, your own site should be the first thing they see.
