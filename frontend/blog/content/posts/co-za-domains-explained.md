Summary: A plain-English guide to .co.za domains — who can register one, what it costs, and how to connect it to your website and email.

`.co.za` is South Africa's most popular domain ending. It tells customers at a glance that you're a local business — and search engines use it as a signal when someone in South Africa searches.

## Who can register a .co.za?

Anyone can — you don't need a registered company. The domain is registered in the owner's name, so use your own (or your business's) real details.

## What does it cost?

Around R150 for the first year through Vicinic, and it's **included free with every paid plan**. Other endings like `.com`, `.africa` or `.org.za` are available too.

## Connecting it to your site

If you buy the domain through Vicinic, there's nothing to do — your site switches over automatically once the domain is live. Already own one? Connect it from the **Deploy** tab and follow the two nameserver steps.

## Matching email

Once your domain is active you can forward `hello@yourbusiness.co.za` to the Gmail or Outlook inbox you already use — no new mailbox to check.
