Customers ask the same questions all day: what time do you open, do you deliver, how much is it? Every paid Vicinic site includes an AI assistant that answers those questions for you, day or night.

## Website chat

A chat bubble sits in the corner of your site. The assistant answers from your own site's content, so it quotes your prices and hours, not made-up ones. On templates that already have a back-to-top or WhatsApp button, the chat bubble stacks neatly above it.

## WhatsApp bot

On Growth Hub and Business OS you can connect your own WhatsApp Business number. The same assistant then replies to customers on WhatsApp, the app most South Africans already use every day.

## How it's built

Replies are generated with Groq's fast open-weight models, and the system switches models automatically if one is unavailable. WhatsApp runs on Meta's official WhatsApp Cloud API, connected through Meta's embedded sign-up, so you never have to hand us a password.
