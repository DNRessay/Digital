Semblance started with a simple question: what would a personal assistant look like if it actually remembered who you are?

## What makes it different

- **Permanent memory.** It keeps a long-term memory, so Semblance can recall what matters instead of starting fresh each time.
- **A team of agents.** Bigger jobs are split between specialised helpers for planning, scheduling, research and more.
- **Personality and emotion.** It adapts to your style and mood, so replies fit the person and the moment.
- **Tools.** It can search the web, follow the news, manage your calendar and message on WhatsApp.
- **An interface that evolves.** The React interface moves through animated stages as the assistant grows.

## Where it leads

Semblance is our research ground for assistants that remember and adapt. Lessons from it go straight into the AI assistant on Vicinic sites.
